BETTER WITH YOU is a fun, focused and forward-thinking electronic music event based in Cape Town, South Africa. Accompanied by immersive sound and set design, we offer community driven nights (and days) with a quality focus and shared values reflected in our lineups, crews and partnerships.

Our events are controlled and respectful environments. No harassment, no discriminiation, no unwanted contact and no intimidation is tolated. Anyone found guilty of violating these rules will be removed and banned from all future events.
